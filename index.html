About

I started at Deloitte in QA and content operations on Adobe Experience Manager — close to the dev side, comfortable with SDLC and system logic. Six months in, I moved internally into the Cyber GRC practice as a TPRM Analyst. That transition wasn't accidental. The CS background and QA mindset translated directly: I already understood how systems fail, how access controls work, and how to document findings that engineers actually act on.

At Deloitte, I run vendor onboarding intake calls, manage periodic risk reassessments on IBM OpenPages, monitor third-party risk signals through Interos and RapidRatings, and participate in control testing. The work I'm most proud of: validating an AI tool's automated SOC 2 control extraction against AICPA Trust Services Criteria — benchmarking AI output against human-reviewer judgment, including subservice org identification and carve-out handling. That's AI governance in practice, not as a slide deck concept.

I'm building toward internal GRC ownership at a product or technology company — designing risk programs, not just executing assessments. The PaySphere Simulation below is the structured proof of that capability: 9 portfolio deliverables across ISO 27001, NIST CSF 2.0, SOC 2, AI governance, regulatory compliance, and full GRC program design.
